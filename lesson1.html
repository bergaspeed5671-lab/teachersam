The Hotel Above the Clouds

High above a mountain valley in Switzerland, a small hotel called Nimbus Retreat sits nestled among ancient pine forests, reachable only by a cable car that climbs slowly through the clouds. Guests arrive expecting a view, but most are surprised by the silence. Unlike crowded city hotels, Nimbus Retreat has just twelve capsule-shaped rooms, each perched on a wooden platform overlooking the valley below.

Every capsule includes a glass ceiling for stargazing, a compact kitchenette, a hot tub, and heated floors, so guests stay cozy even when snow falls outside. In the morning, the clouds often settle beneath the hotel, creating the sensation of floating above the world — a breathtaking sight that guests describe as unforgettable.

There is no spa, no gym, and no nightlife nearby. Instead, guests spend their days hiking quiet trails, reading by the fire, or simply watching the sky change colour. Many visitors say the hotel's greatest luxury is its peaceful silence, something increasingly rare in modern life.

Despite its remote location, Nimbus Retreat is fully booked more than a year in advance — proof that some travellers will go to extraordinary lengths for a night above the clouds.

Grammar — Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

Present Simple

We use the Present Simple for habits, daily routines, facts, things that are generally true, and schedules.

Positive: Subject + verb (+ s/es) — I work in an office. She lives in Canada.

Negative: Subject + do/does not + base verb — I don't work on Sundays. He doesn't like coffee.

Questions: Do/Does + subject + base verb? — Do you travel often? Does she work from home?

Signal words:

alwaysusuallyoften sometimesneverevery day every weekon Mondays

Present Continuous

We use the Present Continuous for something happening now, temporary situations, changes, and future arrangements.

Positive: Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing — I am reading. She is working today.

Negative: Subject + am/is/are not + verb-ing — I'm not sleeping. They aren't travelling today.

Questions: Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing? — Are you listening? Is he driving?

Signal words:

nowright nowcurrently todaythis weekat the moment

Compare

Present SimplePresent Continuous
I work every day.I am working today.
She travels every summer.She is travelling this week.
We visit our parents every weekend.We are visiting them this weekend.
He drives to work.He is driving right now.
